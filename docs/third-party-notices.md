## Country names from iso-codes

The country-name mapping in `aegis/geodata.py` derives from the iso-codes ISO 3166-1 data. Display names are shortened for selected countries; Kosovo (XK) is an additional entry. The continent grouping derives from the supplied Karanik GeoIP Feed v4, with IO included in Asia.
